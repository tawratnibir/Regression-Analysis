3. Mathematical Methodology

Adds a squared \(\ell_2\) penalty on standardized slopes (leaving \(\beta_0\) unpenalized) to stabilize ill-conditioned \(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\tilde{\mathbf{X}}\) and reduce estimator variance.

$$\min_{\beta_0 \in \mathbb{R},\, \boldsymbol{\beta} \in \mathbb{R}^p} \|\mathbf{y} - \beta_0\mathbf{1} - \tilde{\mathbf{X}}\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2 + \alpha\|\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2, \qquad \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\alpha} = (\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}} + \alpha \mathbf{I}_p)^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{y}} \quad (\lambda \equiv \alpha \ge 0)$$

Implementation Note: min_{beta0, beta} ||y - beta0 - X*beta||_2^2 + alpha * ||beta||_2^2 (intercept beta0 unpenalized)
CV-Selected Hyperparameter: \(\alpha^* = 31.0117\) (\(\lambda \equiv \alpha\)) over 60 grid points in \([0.0001, 1e+05]\).

7. Limitations and Conclusions

Facts & Interpretations

  • [Computed Fact] Held-out test performance (\(n_{\mathrm{test}} = 104\)): \(R^2 = 0.7004\), \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.2663\) probability P(Positive) (Train \(R^2 = 0.6363\), Train \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.2934\) probability P(Positive)).
  • [Computed Fact] Largest standardized coefficients by magnitude: polyuria (+0.1361), polydipsia (+0.1347), gender (-0.1259).
  • [Computed Fact] StratifiedKFold(5, seed=42) selected \(\alpha^* = 31.01\) (CV RMSE = 0.3069 probability P(Positive), CV \(R^2 = 0.6009\)), matching closed-form SVD within 9.16e-16.
  • [Interpretation] \(\ell_2\) regularization trades controlled bias for variance reduction, shrinking the standardized \(\ell_2\) norm from 0.2852 (OLS) to 0.2662 (ratio = 0.9332) and reducing effective degrees of freedom from \(p = 16\) to \(\operatorname{df}(\alpha^*) = 14.40\).
  • [Computed Fact] Adding \(\alpha^* \mathbf{I}_p\) stabilized the centered Gram condition number from \(\kappa(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\tilde{\mathbf{X}}) = 11.84\) to \(\kappa(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\tilde{\mathbf{X}} + \alpha^*\mathbf{I}_p) = 9.792\).

Assumptions & Limitations

  • [No Feature Elimination] Ridge shrinks coefficients smoothly toward zero via \(\ell_2\) regularization and never sets coefficients exactly to zero; all \(p\) predictors remain active.
  • [Heteroskedasticity / Nonlinearity] Spearman \(r_s(|\mathbf{e}|, \hat{\mathbf{y}}) = -0.254\) (\(p = 1.51e-07\)).
  • [Linear Probability Model Limitation] 30 of 104 test predictions (28.85%) lie outside \([0, 1]\) (range [-0.042, 1.375]).
  • [Linearity Assumption] Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and conditional target expectation.
