6. Algorithm-Specific Interpretation

  • On the held-out test split (n=80), the model achieves R²=0.8449 and RMSE=2.888 mpg (compared to Train R²=0.8189, Train RMSE=3.37 mpg).
  • Largest standardized predictor effects in absolute magnitude: weight (-5.765), model_year (+2.952), displacement (+1.853).
  • Training-only KFold(5, shuffle=True, seed=42) selected alpha=0.651 (CV RMSE=3.508 mpg, CV R²=0.7931), matching the closed-form SVD solution within 2.66e-14.
  • L2 regularization shrunk the standardized coefficient L2 norm from 7.125 (OLS) to 6.94 (ratio=0.9740), reducing effective predictor degrees of freedom from 8 to 7.88.
  • Adding alpha*I stabilized the centered X^T X eigenvalue condition ratio from 140.1 to 131.8.

7. Limitations and Potential Assumption Violations

  • Ridge regression shrinks all coefficients toward zero smoothly but never produces exact sparsity (all predictors remain active).
  • Residual magnitude correlates with fitted values (Spearman r=0.151, p=6.99e-03), indicating heteroskedasticity or remaining nonlinearity.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
