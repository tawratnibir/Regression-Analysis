3. Mathematical Methodology

Fits an unpenalized hyperplane minimizing the training residual sum of squares via SVD (numpy.linalg.lstsq) on standardized predictors \(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}\) with an unscaled intercept column \(\mathbf{1}\).

$$\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta} \in \mathbb{R}^{k}} \|\mathbf{y} - \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2 = \mathbf{e}^\top\mathbf{e}, \qquad \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}} = [\mathbf{1},\, \tilde{\mathbf{X}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k},\ k = p + 1, \qquad (\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{y}$$

Implementation Note: min_{beta0, beta} ||y - beta0 - X*beta||_2^2 via numpy.linalg.lstsq (intercept unscaled/unpenalized)

7. Limitations and Conclusions

Facts & Interpretations

  • [Computed Fact] Held-out test performance (\(n_{\mathrm{test}} = 1235\)): \(R^2 = -0.5798\), \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 11.44\) UPDRS points (Train \(R^2 = 0.2270\), Train \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 9.69\) UPDRS points).
  • [Computed Fact] Largest standardized coefficients by magnitude: Shimmer:APQ3 (-783.7), Shimmer:DDA (+779.6), Jitter:DDP (+169.8).
  • [Computed Fact] Exact normal-equation identities hold numerically: \(\max |\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{e}| = 1.65e-09\), \(|\bar{y} - \bar{\hat{y}}| = 1.53e-13\), and predictions match scikit-learn within 7.67e-12.
  • [Interpretation] Predictor standardization changes the augmented condition number from \(\kappa(\mathbf{X}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 4.8392e+07\) (unstandardized) to \(\kappa(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 2.432e+04\) (standardized), with \(\operatorname{rank}(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 20\) of \(k = 20\).

Assumptions & Limitations

  • [Multicollinearity] 12 predictor(s) have \(\mathrm{VIF}_j \gt 10\) (max finite VIF = 2.5607e+07), inflating OLS estimator variance \(\sigma^2(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})^{-1}\).
  • [Assumption Violation — Homoskedasticity] Breusch-Pagan test rejects constant disturbance variance (\(p = 3.17e-63\)); classical standard errors require White's sandwich estimator \((\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{e}\mathbf{e}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})^{-1}\).
  • [Assumption Check — Normality] Residuals deviate from Gaussian normality (Jarque-Bera \(p = 8.78e-19\), skewness = 0.234, excess kurtosis = -0.459).
  • [Influential Observations] 131 training row(s) exceed Cook's \(D_i \gt 4/n\) (max = 0.04563).
  • [Assumption Violation — No Autocorrelation] Durbin-Watson \(\mathrm{DW} = 0.116\) (lag-1 \(\hat{\rho}_1(e) = 0.942\)), indicating correlated errors.
  • [Group Generalization] Evaluation tests unseen 'subject#' clusters without subject-specific random intercepts.
  • [Generalization Gap] Test \(R^2\) (-0.5798) drops from Train \(R^2\) (0.2270), indicating distribution shift or limited linear generalization.
  • [Linearity Assumption] Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and conditional target expectation.
