3. Mathematical Methodology

Fits an unpenalized hyperplane minimizing the training residual sum of squares via SVD (numpy.linalg.lstsq) on standardized predictors \(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}\) with an unscaled intercept column \(\mathbf{1}\).

$$\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta} \in \mathbb{R}^{k}} \|\mathbf{y} - \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2 = \mathbf{e}^\top\mathbf{e}, \qquad \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}} = [\mathbf{1},\, \tilde{\mathbf{X}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k},\ k = p + 1, \qquad (\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{y}$$

Implementation Note: min_{beta0, beta} ||y - beta0 - X*beta||_2^2 via numpy.linalg.lstsq (intercept unscaled/unpenalized)

7. Limitations and Conclusions

Facts & Interpretations

  • [Computed Fact] Held-out test performance (\(n_{\mathrm{test}} = 104\)): \(R^2 = 0.6974\), \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.2676\) probability P(Positive) (Train \(R^2 = 0.6379\), Train \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.2928\) probability P(Positive)).
  • [Computed Fact] Largest standardized coefficients by magnitude: polydipsia (+0.1446), polyuria (+0.1426), gender (-0.138).
  • [Computed Fact] Exact normal-equation identities hold numerically: \(\max |\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{e}| = 2.04e-13\), \(|\bar{y} - \bar{\hat{y}}| = 5.55e-16\), and predictions match scikit-learn within 1.78e-15.
  • [Interpretation] Predictor standardization changes the augmented condition number from \(\kappa(\mathbf{X}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 230.9\) (unstandardized) to \(\kappa(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 3.442\) (standardized), with \(\operatorname{rank}(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}) = 17\) of \(k = 17\).

Assumptions & Limitations

  • [Assumption Violation — Homoskedasticity] Breusch-Pagan test rejects constant disturbance variance (\(p = 1.89e-07\)); classical standard errors require White's sandwich estimator \((\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \mathbf{e}\mathbf{e}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{X}}_{\mathrm{aug}})^{-1}\).
  • [Assumption Check — Normality] Residuals deviate from Gaussian normality (Jarque-Bera \(p = 3.89e-07\), skewness = 0.649, excess kurtosis = -0.129).
  • [Influential Observations] 23 training row(s) exceed Cook's \(D_i \gt 4/n\) (max = 0.03343).
  • [Assumption Violation — No Autocorrelation] Durbin-Watson \(\mathrm{DW} = 1.033\) (lag-1 \(\hat{\rho}_1(e) = 0.477\)), indicating correlated errors.
  • [Linear Probability Model Limitation] 31 of 104 test predictions (29.81%) lie outside \([0, 1]\) (range [-0.070, 1.443]).
  • [Linearity Assumption] Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and conditional target expectation.
