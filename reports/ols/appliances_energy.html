7. Limitations and Potential Assumption Violations

  • Rank-deficient design matrix (rank 27 < 28): exact collinearity is present (e.g., identical features), so individual OLS coefficients are not uniquely identified even though lstsq returns the minimum-norm solution.
  • Substantial multicollinearity detected: 15 predictor(s) have VIF > 10 (max finite VIF=233.75), inflating OLS coefficient variance.
  • Heteroskedasticity check rejects constant error variance (Breusch-Pagan p=1.72e-104), meaning classical homoskedastic OLS standard errors would be unreliable.
  • Training residuals deviate from Gaussian normality (Jarque-Bera p=0.00e+00, skewness=3.283, excess kurtosis=14.189).
  • 922 training observation(s) exceed the Cook's distance 4/n threshold (max Cook's D=0.009242), indicating potential sensitivity to influential points.
  • Residual autocorrelation is indicated (Durbin-Watson=0.622, lag-1 corr=0.689), violating independent-error assumptions—especially notable for ordered/time-series data.
  • Purely linear static features without explicit lag or seasonal terms may miss temporal dynamics across the chronological train-to-test shift.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
