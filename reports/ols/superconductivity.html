  • Heteroskedasticity check rejects constant error variance (Breusch-Pagan p=0.00e+00), meaning classical homoskedastic OLS standard errors would be unreliable.
  • Training residuals deviate from Gaussian normality (Jarque-Bera p=0.00e+00, skewness=-0.350, excess kurtosis=1.717).
  • 636 training observation(s) exceed the Cook's distance 4/n threshold (max Cook's D=0.04195), indicating potential sensitivity to influential points.
  • Residual autocorrelation is indicated (Durbin-Watson=1.019, lag-1 corr=0.490), violating independent-error assumptions—especially notable for ordered/time-series data.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
