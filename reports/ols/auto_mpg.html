7. Limitations and Potential Assumption Violations

  • Substantial multicollinearity detected: 3 predictor(s) have VIF > 10 (max finite VIF=22.817), inflating OLS coefficient variance.
  • Heteroskedasticity check rejects constant error variance (Breusch-Pagan p=3.70e-04), meaning classical homoskedastic OLS standard errors would be unreliable.
  • Training residuals deviate from Gaussian normality (Jarque-Bera p=2.94e-05, skewness=0.417, excess kurtosis=0.938).
  • 23 training observation(s) exceed the Cook's distance 4/n threshold (max Cook's D=0.1145), indicating potential sensitivity to influential points.
  • Residual autocorrelation is indicated (Durbin-Watson=1.220, lag-1 corr=0.389), violating independent-error assumptions—especially notable for ordered/time-series data.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
