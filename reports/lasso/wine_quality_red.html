3. Mathematical Methodology

Adds an \(\ell_1\) penalty on standardized slopes (leaving \(\beta_0\) unpenalized) to perform continuous shrinkage and automatic variable selection via coordinate-wise soft-thresholding.

$$\min_{\beta_0 \in \mathbb{R},\, \boldsymbol{\beta} \in \mathbb{R}^p} \frac{1}{2n}\|\mathbf{y} - \beta_0\mathbf{1} - \tilde{\mathbf{X}}\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2 + \alpha\|\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_1, \qquad \hat{\beta}_j = \mathcal{S}\!\left(\frac{1}{n}\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_j^\top \mathbf{r}^{(-j)}, \alpha\right) \quad \left(\lambda \equiv \alpha \ge 0,\ \frac{1}{n}\|\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_j\|_2^2 = 1\right)$$

Implementation Note: min_{beta0, beta} (1 / (2*n)) * ||y - beta0 - X*beta||_2^2 + alpha * ||beta||_1 (intercept beta0 unpenalized)
CV-Selected Hyperparameter: \(\alpha^* = 0.00899704\) (\(\lambda \equiv \alpha\)) over 60 grid points in \([3.813e-05, 0.3813]\).

7. Limitations and Conclusions

Facts & Interpretations

  • [Computed Fact] Held-out test performance (\(n_{\mathrm{test}} = 320\)): \(R^2 = 0.3990\), \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.6267\) score (0-10 ordinal) (Train \(R^2 = 0.3461\), Train \(\mathrm{RMSE} = 0.6522\) score (0-10 ordinal)).
  • [Computed Fact] Largest standardized coefficients by magnitude: alcohol (+0.2985), volatile acidity (-0.1713), sulphates (+0.1326).
  • [Computed Fact] KFold(5, shuffle=True, seed=42) selected \(\alpha^* = 0.008997\) (CV RMSE = 0.6603 score (0-10 ordinal), CV \(R^2 = 0.3281\)), converging in 26 coordinate-descent iterations.
  • [Interpretation] Soft-thresholding retained \(|A(\alpha^*)| = 8\) of \(p = 11\) predictors and eliminated 3 (27.3% sparse), reducing the \(\ell_1\) norm from 1.047 (OLS) to 0.8751 (ratio = 0.8362).
  • [Computed Fact] Eliminated predictors include citric acid, residual sugar, density (max \(|r|\) with a retained predictor = 0.669).

Assumptions & Limitations

  • [Selection Instability Under Collinearity] 3 predictor(s) varied in selection across CV folds (e.g., residual sugar (0.40), citric acid (0.20), density (0.20)), because \(\ell_1\) KKT pinning selects one representative among correlated features.
  • [Ordinal Target Assumption] 'quality' is an integer ordinal score modeled continuously.
  • [Linearity Assumption] Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and conditional target expectation.
