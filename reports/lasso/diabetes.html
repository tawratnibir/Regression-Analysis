3. Mathematical Methodology

Adds an L1 penalty to perform continuous shrinkage and automatic variable selection via coordinate-wise soft-thresholding.

$$\min_{\beta_0 \in \mathbb{R},\, \boldsymbol{\beta} \in \mathbb{R}^p} \frac{1}{2n}\|\mathbf{y} - \beta_0\mathbf{1} - \tilde{\mathbf{X}}\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2 + \alpha\|\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_1, \qquad \hat{\beta}_j = \mathcal{S}\!\left(\frac{1}{n}\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_j^\top \mathbf{r}^{(-j)}, \alpha\right) \quad (\lambda \equiv \alpha)$$

Implementation Note: min_{beta0, beta} (1 / (2*n)) * ||y - beta0 - X*beta||_2^2 + alpha * ||beta||_1 (intercept beta0 unpenalized)
CV-Selected Hyperparameter: \(\alpha^* = 0.00463341\) over 60 grid points in \([3.136e-05, 0.3136]\).

  • [Interpretation] Soft-thresholding retained 15 of 16 predictors and eliminated 1 (6.2% sparse), reducing the L1 norm from 0.8527 (OLS) to 0.7787 (ratio = 0.9132).
  • [Computed Fact] Eliminated predictors include age (max |corr| with a retained predictor = 0.401).

Assumptions & Limitations

  • [Selection Instability Under Collinearity] 4 predictor(s) varied in selection across CV folds (e.g., muscle_stiffness (0.80), alopecia (0.80), weakness (0.60), age (0.40)), because L1 KKT pinning selects one representative among correlated features.
  • [Linear Probability Model Limitation] 30 of 104 test predictions (28.85%) lie outside [0, 1] (range [-0.024, 1.423]).
  • [Linearity Assumption] Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and conditional target expectation.
