6. Algorithm-Specific Interpretation

  • On the held-out test split (n=83), the model achieves R²=0.6839 and RMSE=7.282 10,000 NTD/Ping (compared to Train R²=0.5574, Train RMSE=9.124 10,000 NTD/Ping).
  • Largest standardized predictor effects in absolute magnitude: X3 distance to the nearest MRT station (-5.358), X4 number of convenience stores (+3.161), X2 house age (-2.911).
  • Training-only KFold(5, shuffle=True, seed=42) selected alpha=0.1331 (CV RMSE=9.212 10,000 NTD/Ping, CV R²=0.5437), converging in 15 coordinate-descent iterations.
  • Lasso retained 5 of 6 predictors and set 1 exactly to zero (16.7% sparsity), shrinking the L1 norm from 16.89 (OLS) to 15.62 (ratio=0.9248).
  • Examples of eliminated predictors: X6 longitude (max |corr| with a retained predictor among eliminated features = 0.792).

7. Limitations and Potential Assumption Violations

  • Feature-selection instability across CV folds: 1 feature(s) were selected in some folds but zeroed in others (e.g., X6 longitude (0.40)), a known consequence of L1 regularization under correlated predictors.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
