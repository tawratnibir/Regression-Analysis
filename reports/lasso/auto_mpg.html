6. Algorithm-Specific Interpretation

  • On the held-out test split (n=80), the model achieves R²=0.8410 and RMSE=2.924 mpg (compared to Train R²=0.8143, Train RMSE=3.412 mpg).
  • Largest standardized predictor effects in absolute magnitude: weight (-4.9), model_year (+2.8), origin_2 (+0.7262).
  • Training-only KFold(5, shuffle=True, seed=42) selected alpha=0.09678 (CV RMSE=3.495 mpg, CV R²=0.7947), converging in 45 coordinate-descent iterations.
  • Lasso retained 5 of 8 predictors and set 3 exactly to zero (37.5% sparsity), shrinking the L1 norm from 14.14 (OLS) to 9.314 (ratio=0.6589).
  • Examples of eliminated predictors: cylinders, displacement, acceleration (max |corr| with a retained predictor among eliminated features = 0.929).

7. Limitations and Potential Assumption Violations

  • Feature-selection instability across CV folds: 2 feature(s) were selected in some folds but zeroed in others (e.g., horsepower (0.60), acceleration (0.20)), a known consequence of L1 regularization under correlated predictors.
  • Linear models assume additive linear relationships between standardized predictors and the conditional target mean; nonlinear interactions require basis expansion or nonlinear models.
